A more approachable way to swim

BlueCurrent Swim Co. was imagined by fictional swim coach Maya Bennett, a former collegiate swimmer who believes good training begins with a clear plan. Our collection emphasizes practical instructions, achievable goals, and the freedom to adjust a session to your comfort level.

Every plan on this website is free to read. You do not need special equipment or a competitive swimming background to get started.
